# Brand — OffboardAI

Status: active

## Direction

Premium dark editorial B2B SaaS for CTOs and Engineering Managers. The page should feel technical, calm, and high-stakes rather than playful.

## Palette

- Background: near-black blue `#020408`
- Surface: elevated blue-black `#0A0F1A`
- Surface 2: deep slate `#111827`
- Primary accent: signal green `#4ADE80`
- Secondary accent: restrained indigo `#818CF8`
- Text: soft slate-white `#F1F5F9`
- Muted text: cool slate `#94A3B8`

## Typography

- Display: Syne, bold and editorial
- Body: DM Sans, compact and readable
- Mono: JetBrains Mono, used for metrics, Markdown, and technical labels

## Voice

- Direct, concrete, CTO-facing.
- Lead with operational risk, continuity, and artifact quality.
- Avoid vague AI claims; describe the delivered document and workflow.
